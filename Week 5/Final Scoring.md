##  Final Evaluation

After completing End Phase Settlement, the system should not only display Final USD Wealth and Net P&L, but should also help the player understand

- how much profit / loss was generated by asset investment decisions;
- whether using JPY as a funding source created benefits or costs;
- how well the player managed liquidity and debt exposure;
- which decisions were reasonable;
- which decisions need improvement.

### Debrief Flow

Final Result
      ↓
P&L Breakdown
      ↓
Performance Feedback
      ↓
Score

> **Important:** Evaluation must be based on the information available at the time the player made the decision, and must not use outcomes from later rounds to retrospectively judge an earlier decision.

---

## 1.1. Final Result

The first section displays the overall result.

| Output | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Final USD Wealth** | USD remaining after liquidating assets and repaying all JPY debt |
| **Final Status** | Phase Complete / Bankrupt |
| **Net P&L** | Total profit / loss of the entire Phase |
| **Total Score** | Overall evaluation score out of 100 |

### UI Example

```text
FINAL RESULT
────────────────────────

Final USD Wealth
$6,500

Net P&L
-$1,420

Final Status
PHASE COMPLETE

Total Score
72 / 100
```

---

## 1.2. P&L Breakdown

P&L is separated into three parts so that the player knows **where the final result came from**.

The breakdown is based on:

```text
Investment P&L
+
Funding P&L
-
Information Cost
=
Net P&L
```

| Component | Content | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **Investment P&L** | Tech P&L + S&P 500 P&L + US Cash Interest | Result from USD investment decisions |
| **Funding P&L** | FX P&L − Interest Cost | Result from using JPY as a funding source |
| **Information Cost** | Total fees paid for additional information | Cost of obtaining additional information signals |
| **Net P&L** | Investment P&L + Funding P&L − Information Cost | Final result of the strategy |

### Example

| P&L Component | Result |
|---|---:|
| Investment P&L | +$1,200 |
| Funding P&L | -$700 |
| Information Cost | -$100 |
| **NET P&L** | **+$400** |

---

### Investment P&L Breakdown

| Component | Result |
|---|---:|
| US Tech P&L | +$850 |
| S&P 500 P&L | +$250 |
| US Cash Interest | +$100 |
| **Investment P&L** | **+$1,200** |

---

### Funding P&L Breakdown

| Component | Result |
|---|---:|
| FX P&L | -$620 |
| Interest Cost | -$80 |
| **Funding P&L** | **-$700** |

This output helps the player clearly understand:

> **Asset investment can be profitable while the overall strategy can still lose money if JPY appreciates or funding cost becomes too large.**

## 1.3. What You Did Well

Feedback should not only state:

```text
Good Decision
```

The system must clearly show:

```text
Decision
  ↓
Financial / Risk Impact
```

### Examples

| Category | Feedback |
|---|---|
| **Debt Management** | ✓ You reduced JPY debt exposure before funding risk increased further. |
| **Liquidity Management** | ✓ You maintained enough Free Cash to meet repayment obligations. |
| **US Cash Use** | ✓ You moved part of your capital into US Cash during a higher-risk market environment. |
| **Debt Timing** | ✓ You repaid an outstanding loan instead of maintaining unnecessary JPY exposure. |
| **Information Use** | ✓ You used additional information to support a relevant market decision. |
| **Trading Discipline** | ✓ Your orders remained within available cash and current holdings. |

The system should select approximately:

```text
2–4 feedback
```

items that are most meaningful based on the player's decision history.

---

## 1.4. What Could Be Improved

Improvement feedback must also be linked to specific behavior.

| Category | Feedback |
|---|---|
| **High JPY Debt Exposure** | ✗ Your strategy maintained a large JPY debt position, increasing FX and funding risk. |
| **Weak Liquidity** | ✗ Your available liquidity became low relative to upcoming repayment obligations. |
| **Risky Position** | ✗ A large share of invested capital remained exposed to a risky asset during a stressed market environment. |
| **Idle Borrowed Cash** | ✗ You borrowed additional JPY but left a large amount of Free Cash unused. |
| **Information Use** | ✗ Information cost reduced Net P&L without leading to a meaningful change in strategy. |
| **Repayment Preparation** | ✗ Available liquidity was insufficient when repayment was required. |
| **Repeated Invalid Actions** | ✗ Several attempted actions exceeded available cash, holdings or borrowing capacity. |

### Do Not Use Hindsight

Do not use:

> ✗ You should have sold Tech before Round 3 because Tech later crashed.

Instead use:

> ✗ Your portfolio entered the next round with a large risky Tech position, increasing market exposure.

This means the system evaluates:

> **decision quality at the time the decision was made**

instead of using future outcomes to judge the player.

# 2. Scoring System

Total score:

```text
100 POINTS
```

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| **Financial Performance** | 50 |
| **Risk Management** | 30 |
| **Decision Quality** | 20 |
| **TOTAL** | **100** |

---

## 12.1. Financial Performance — 50 Points

Absolute USD amounts should not be used alone because the roles have different borrowing capacities.

For example:

- Speculator has a higher borrowing capacity;
- Conservative Investor has a lower borrowing capacity.

Therefore, use:

```text
Net P&L Return =
Net P&L / Total USD Funding Used
```

### Financial Performance Scoring

| Net P&L Return | Score |
|---|---:|
| ≥ +20% | 50 |
| +15% → < +20% | 47 |
| +10% → < +15% | 44 |
| +5% → < +10% | 38–41 |
| 0% → < +5% | 32–35 |
| -5% → < 0% | 24–28 |
| -10% → < -5% | 20 |
| -20% → < -10% | 10–15 |
| < -20% | 5 |
| Bankruptcy | 0 |

> Final thresholds can be adjusted after scenario testing.

---

## 2.2. Risk Management — 30 Points

Risk Management evaluates three factors:

| Metric | Maximum Score |
|---|---:|
| **Liquidity Management** | 10 |
| **Debt Exposure Management** | 10 |
| **Position Risk Management** | 10 |
| **TOTAL** | **30** |

---

### A. Liquidity Management — 10 Points

Use:

```text
Liquidity Coverage =
Available Liquidity / Debt Repayment Due
```

A player with sufficient liquidity to meet repayment obligations receives a higher score.

Risk is considered high when:

```text
Available Liquidity
<
Debt Repayment Due
```

---

### B. Debt Exposure Management — 10 Points

Use:

```text
Debt Exposure =
USD Debt MTM / Gross Portfolio Value
```

Debt that is too large relative to the portfolio:

```text
High Debt Exposure
→
Lower Risk Management Score
```

This metric reflects the level of dependence on JPY funding.

---

### C. Position Risk Management — 10 Points

The system evaluates the **position created by the player through BUY / SELL / HOLD**.

Example:

```text
Risky Position Concentration =
Largest Risky Asset Value
/
Total Invested Portfolio
```

If almost the entire portfolio is concentrated in one risky asset:

```text
High Concentration
→
Lower Position Risk Score
```

---

## 2.3. Decision Quality — 20 Points

| Metric | Maximum Score |
|---|---:|
| **Capital Efficiency** | 5 |
| **Debt Timing** | 5 |
| **Information Use** | 5 |
| **Action Discipline** | 5 |
| **TOTAL** | **20** |

---

### A. Capital Efficiency — 5 Points

Evaluate whether the player borrows JPY but leaves too much **idle Free Cash**.

Use:

```text
Idle Cash Ratio =
Unused Free Cash / Borrowed USD
```

If borrowed capital is used efficiently:

```text
Lower Idle Cash Ratio
→
Higher Capital Efficiency Score
```

---

### B. Debt Timing — 5 Points

Evaluate:

- when additional borrowing occurs;
- how long a loan is held;
- timing of REPAY;
- whether unnecessary debt is maintained.

Do not evaluate based on:

> "knowing in advance where FX will move."

The evaluation must be based on market information available at the time of the decision.

---

### C. Information Use — 5 Points

Purchasing information does not automatically earn points.

A high score is given when:

> **information is purchased and actually supports a logical decision.**

If the player:

```text
SKIP INFORMATION
```

but still makes a reasonable decision, the player can still receive a high score.

The Information score evaluates **quality of use**, not the amount of information purchased.

---

### D. Action Discipline — 5 Points

Based on the number of invalid actions made by the player.

Examples:

- BUY exceeds Free Cash;
- SELL exceeds holdings;
- WITHDRAW exceeds US Cash;
- borrowing exceeds role cap;
- negative quantity;
- invalid repayment action.

Logic:

```text
Fewer Invalid Actions
→
Higher Action Discipline Score
```

# 4. Key Lesson

Finally, the system creates a short takeaway based on the player's actual result.

### Case 1 — Investment Is Profitable but Funding P&L Is Significantly Negative

> **Your investments made money, but the stronger yen and funding costs erased those gains.**

---

### Case 2 — Player Manages Debt Well

> **Reducing JPY exposure helped protect your gains when funding conditions became less favorable.**

---

### Case 3 — Player Borrows Heavily but Leaves Idle Cash

> **Borrowing only creates value when the capital is used efficiently enough to cover its funding and FX risks.**

---

### Case 4 — Player Is Bankrupt

> **Return matters, but liquidity determines whether the strategy survives.**



but to help the player understand:

```text

      ↓
Which decision contributed to it?
      ↓
What risk was involved?
      ↓
What could be done better next time?
```
