      How to play — JPY Carry Trade

      4 rounds, 5 minutes each. You start with $0 — borrowing JPY is your only source of capital.
      1

      Each round shows 2 base news stories; you can purchase 1 additional story. Information Hunters alone see each story's impact level on next round's rate, FX, and prices.

      2

      Borrow JPY (25/50/75/100% packages) and buy US Tech, S&P 500, or US Cash. Repaying a loan frees up your borrowing cap again.

      3

      Next, optionally sell holdings, withdraw US Cash, or repay a loan in full — then lock in before time runs out.

      4

      Each round updates prices, FX, and interest. After round 4, everything is auto-liquidated and JPY debt is settled at the final rate.

      5

      Your Final Evaluation splits Investment from Funding P&L and scores Financial Performance, Risk Management, and Decision Quality.
