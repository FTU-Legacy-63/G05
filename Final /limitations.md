
# Arbiverse — Limitations and Future Development

## 1. Opportunities for Future Development

| Area | Current Design Focus | Potential Future Enhancement |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Tulip Mania** | Historical speculation illustrated through NPC offers, market news, and decisions to trade or hold cash. | Introduce alternative price trajectories, adaptive NPC behavior, and additional information-value scenarios to increase replayability. |
| **Phase 2 — FX Arbitrage** | Three-leg currency conversion, market information, capital allocation, and onshore/offshore market differences. | Add variable bid–ask spreads, transfer constraints, and execution conditions for a more advanced arbitrage simulation. |
| **Phase 3 — Carry Trade** | JPY funding, USD asset allocation, FX exposure, borrowing costs, and final settlement. | Develop more detailed portfolio exposure tracking, capital-utilization analytics, and scenario-based stress testing. |
| **Scoring & Feedback** | A consistent 100-point framework evaluates profitability, risk management, and decision quality. | Calibrate scoring against a wider set of player strategies and provide more granular post-game explanations. |
| **Game Experience** | Guided rounds and period-specific news interfaces make complex concepts approachable. | Add adjustable difficulty, tutorial modes, accessibility options, and comparative performance dashboards. |
| **Testing & Evaluation** | Logic checks and financial test cases support the current implementation. | Extend structured playtesting across devices and player groups, supported by usability feedback and automated regression tests. |

---

## 2. Development Priorities

1. **Broader scenario coverage:** Provide multiple plausible market developments without changing the educational objectives of each phase.

2. **Richer risk modeling:** Expand market frictions, execution uncertainty, and exposure monitoring for advanced players.

3. **More informative feedback:** Help players understand *why* particular decisions performed well or poorly, beyond the final score.

4. **Player validation:** Gather feedback on clarity, engagement, and learning outcomes to guide subsequent iterations.
