
# User Flow

## 1. Overall Player Journey

Launch Arbiverse

↓

Select Phase 1, 2, or 3 from the main interface

↓

Read the phase introduction / open How to Play

↓

Start the game and review the current market round

↓

Read public news, market indicators, prices, and exchange rates

↓

Optionally purchase additional information

↓

Choose a strategy → Set trade / allocation → Confirm action

↓

Review updated cash, holdings, debt, or transaction details

↓

Finish the round / reach the timer limit

↓

Read round settlement, feedback, and market explanation

↓

Continue to the next round (repeat the decision cycle)

↓

Review final settlement, financial results, score, and lessons

↓

Replay or return to phase selection

The phases are **separate playable market scenarios**, but share a consistent learning cycle: **observe information → evaluate alternatives → act → receive feedback → reassess**. Players are evaluated on both financial outcomes and decision making under the rules of each phase.

## 2. Phase-by-Phase User Flow

### 2.1 Phase 1 — Tulip Mania (1637)

**Initial position:** **$1,000 cash**, **0 tulips**.

**Duration:** **3 rounds**.

| Step | Player action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Enter the market** | Open Phase 1, review instructions, and begin. | The market board opens with the current round, countdown, cash, tulip inventory, estimated wealth, and market indicators. |
| **2. Assess conditions** | Examine prices, **Speculation**, **Liquidity**, **Confidence**, and public market news. | The game provides contextual signals about the evolving tulip market; information changes across rounds. |
| **3. Consider private information** | Optionally purchase a report for **$200**. | The relevant information is revealed and the cost is deducted from available cash. Purchasing information is not required to trade. |
| **4. Choose an action** | Select **Stay in Cash** or **Execute Actions**. | Stay in Cash skips new trades for the round; Execute Actions opens trading opportunities. The interface tracks the two-use cash-stay allowance. |
| **5. Evaluate NPC offers** | Review traders' offers and decide whether to **accept** or **decline** each one. | Each offer identifies the trading side, quantity, and price. Buying requires sufficient cash; selling requires sufficient tulip inventory. |
| **6. Execute and monitor** | Accept a valid buy or sell offer, or decline the remaining offers. | Accepted purchases reduce cash and increase tulips; accepted sales increase cash and reduce tulips. The displayed holdings and wealth update. |
| **7. End the round** | Complete the action stage or allow the timer to expire. | A **Round Debrief** summarizes participation and provides context-specific feedback. The player advances to the next market round. |
| **8. Experience the crash** | Complete Round 3 and select **See the Crash**. | Remaining tulips are valued at the post-crash price of **$15 each**. The game calculates final wealth and overall P&L. |
| **9. Review the outcome** | Examine the scorecard, trader archetype, and lessons. | Final results show financial performance, trading execution, decision quality, risk management, and any activity-related score cap. |

### 2.2 Phase 2 — Thai Baht Crisis (1997)

**Initial position:** **$120,000 USD**.

**Duration:** **4 rounds**.

| Step | Player action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Enter the FX desk** | Start Phase 2 and review the rules, current round, and market board. | The screen displays cash, cumulative P&L, trading cap, fee per leg, market rates, and time remaining. |
| **2. Read available quotes** | Examine public **USD/THB** and **USD/SGD** rates and the related market context. | The **SGD/THB quoted rate is not directly revealed** on the initial rate board, encouraging interpretation of news and implied market relationships. |
| **3. Evaluate information** | Read public telex reports and optionally buy additional information for **$600 per report**. | Paid information becomes available and its cost is deducted from cash; public information is accessible without purchase. |
| **4. Choose participation** | Select **Stay in Cash** or **Execute Action**. | Stay in Cash avoids the round's FX trade and conversion fees, subject to the game's two-use allowance. Execute Action proceeds to allocation and route selection. |
| **5. Set trade capital** | Enter the USD amount to allocate. | Valid allocations follow **1,000increments**,withamaximumof**50,000 per round** and no more than available cash. Invalid amounts cannot be confirmed. |
| **6. Select an FX path** | Choose **Path A or Path B** in R1, R2, and R4. In R3, select from **four cross-market routes (A–D)**. | The conversion pipeline shows the currency sequence and, where applicable, Onshore/Offshore execution venues. |
| **7. Confirm the trade** | Review the allocation and selected route, then confirm. | The engine calculates the three currency conversions, transaction costs for three legs, and any round information expense. |
| **8. Review settlement** | Read the end-of-round result. | The debrief displays gross conversion proceeds, fees, information cost, net P&L, net return, and the updated USD balance carried forward. |
| **9. Interpret the opportunity** | Compare the chosen route with the market explanation. | The settlement reveals the **quoted SGD/THB**, **implied cross-rate**, market discrepancy, and the game-defined best decision for that round. |
| **10. Continue or finish** | Advance through R1–R4; review the final report. | The game summarizes cumulative profit and the **100-point score** across Financial Performance, Risk Management, and Decision Quality. |

**Round-specific decisions:**

| Round | Market condition | Player's main task |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 — First Signs of Divergence** | Early differences between FX relationships. | Interpret modest signals and decide whether and how much to trade. |
| **R2 — The Gap Widens** | Currency pressure and a wider discrepancy. | Reassess the direction of the triangular conversion and capital allocation. |
| **R3 — Two-Tier Market Split** | Different Onshore and Offshore quotes. | Compare four routes, identify the direction of potential cross-market arbitrage, and consider transfer restrictions described in the news. |
| **R4 — Rates Float, Spread Closes** | Quoted and implied relationships approach convergence. | Decide whether a trade can justify its costs or whether preserving cash is preferable. |

### 2.3 Phase 3 — Carry Trade (2024)

**Initial position:** **$0 Free Cash** before borrowing.

**Duration:** **4 rounds plus final settlement**.

| Step | Player action | System response / resulting output |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Review the macro briefing** | Read economic news, **USD/JPY**, BOJ funding rate, US Tech and S&P 500 prices. | The market board shows round-specific data and explains the relevant interest-rate, currency, and asset-market environment. |
| **2. Establish funding** | Select a **JPY borrowing package** in Round 1. | The screen shows the amount borrowed, USD conversion, one-time borrowing fee, and **Net Free Cash Credited**. Round 1 requires a borrowing selection. |
| **3. Consider additional information** | Read available market posts and optionally purchase extra information. | The player receives additional context; any information cost is included in the game's financial accounting. |
| **4. Decide how to allocate** | Use Free Cash to buy **US Tech**, buy **S&P 500**, or deposit into interest-earning **US Cash**. | The system validates the available balance, applicable stock transaction fees, and the selected quantities. |
| **5. Manage positions** | Buy, sell, hold, or rebalance assets as permitted by the round's controls. | Holdings, Free Cash, Gross Portfolio, debt value, and Net Wealth update with recorded decisions and market prices. |
| **6. Manage financing** | Review loan tranches and, when possible, use **Repay in full**. | The game accounts for JPY principal, accrued interest, and the prevailing USD/JPY conversion needed to repay debt. |
| **7. Confirm and close the round** | Complete permitted actions or reach the round timer limit. | The **Market Close** screen summarizes portfolio value, USD debt, Net Wealth, status, actions, and interest earned on US Cash. |
| **8. Adapt to the next market** | Move to the following round and reassess news, rates, prices, and exposure. | The scenario updates asset prices, FX rates, funding conditions, and investment opportunities. |
| **9. Enter final settlement** | Finish Round 4 and proceed to **End Phase Settlement**. | Residual Tech/S&P holdings are liquidated, US Cash is withdrawn, and outstanding JPY loans are settled using the designated final prices and FX rate. |
| **10. Review final results** | Examine financial statements, the scorecard, round history, and educational feedback. | The game presents Investment P&L, FX P&L, borrowing/interest costs, Net P&L, final wealth, score, and a trader archetype. Bankruptcy is shown if applicable. |

**Important distinction:**

- **Free Cash** is immediately available for transactions.
- **US Cash** is a separate interest-earning allocation.
- **Net Wealth** is the marked-to-market value of the player's assets **minus the USD value of outstanding JPY debt**.

## 3. Common Decision Rules and Feedback

| Area | Phase(s) | User-flow rule or outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Optional paid information** | 1–3 | Players may spend part of their resources for additional information; costs reduce financial performance. |
| **Stay in Cash** | 1–2 | Separate action from executing a trade; the interface implements a **two-use limit** for manual cash decisions. |
| **Transaction validation** | 1–3 | Trades must satisfy the applicable cash, inventory, position, borrowing, and allocation requirements. |
| **FX route selection** | 2 | Two standard routes in R1/R2/R4; four cross-market routes in R3. Paths are selected before settlement. |
| **Time limit** | 1–3 | Each round has a countdown; expiry can end or settle the round according to phase-specific logic. |
| **Round-by-round review** | 1–3 | A debrief or Market Close screen explains the result before the player continues. |
| **Final financial results** | 1–3 | Profit and wealth are calculated using the phase-specific asset and funding mechanics. |
| **Final scoring** | 1–3 | A **100-point score** evaluates performance and/or decisions; it is distinct from monetary P&L. |
| **Conditional outcome** | 1–3 | Activity caps, participation notices, or bankruptcy may appear when the relevant conditions are met. |
| **Next phase / replay** | 1–3 | After completing a phase, players can review results and navigate back to other available scenarios. |

## 4. What the Player Learns From the Flow

| Phase | Central learning outcome |
|---|---|
| **Tulip Mania** | Speculation, entry and exit timing, liquidity preservation, and inventory risk during a bubble and crash. |
| **Thai Baht Crisis** | Currency relationships, implied exchange rates, transaction-cost-aware arbitrage, and differences between trading venues. |
| **Carry Trade** | Interest-rate differentials, leveraged investment decisions, foreign-exchange exposure, liquidity management, and debt settlement. |
